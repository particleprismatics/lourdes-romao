About

Lourdes Romão

Lourdes Romão is a visual artist and designer working across painting, sculpture, fused glass, photography and graphic design. Her work moves fluidly between materials — oil on canvas, clay, and layered glass — with a recurring interest in colour, nature and the patterns of everyday life.

Alongside her studio practice she has contributed to numerous fundraising organizations and non-profits, and runs her independent design studio, Azure Design.

MFA, Massachusetts College of Art · BFA, FAAP, São Paulo, Brasil
